TELEGRAM DOWNLOADER · v3.2.0

Your Telegram files, downloaded and organized.

Save groups or selected topics without downloading each file by hand. Keep progress and continue another day.

Created collaboratively by Jhon Anthony and ChatGPT (Codex), an AI from OpenAI. Jhon defined the features and tested versions; the AI wrote the code and publishes it on GitHub with his explicit authorization.

Choose your language

Open ⚙ Settings at the top right of the panel and choose Browser default, English or Español. Your choice is saved. Changing languages keeps queues, topic selections, folders and progress. Telegram content stays in its original language.

Four modes

  • Blue · Open video: save the current video or add it to My queue.
  • Purple · My queue: download your chosen videos in batches.
  • Green · Group / topic: download videos with a batch amount, order and starting point.
  • Amber · Backup: create a group backup organized by topics and file types.

Create a topic backup

  1. Open the group in Telegram Web K.
  2. Open Backup and click Load group topics.
  3. Choose all or some topics, including General if desired.
  4. Choose videos, photos, audio, documents and/or messages with captions.
  5. Click Choose folder & create backup. You can use an external drive.

Each topic gets a folder. Files have date-based names and stable identifiers. Messages are saved as readable text and JSON.

Continue later

Use Continue / update this backup and choose the same backup folder or its parent. Completed files are checked and skipped. An interrupted file restarts from the beginning. Keep Telegram-respaldo.json and the folder structure.

Updating adds what's missing. All topics also includes newly created topics. Earlier local copies are kept if Telegram messages are deleted. Files saved in other locations are not automatically part of this backup.

Speed and disk space

Fast uses up to 8 simultaneous chunks and Intensive up to 16, with ordered disk writes and bounded memory. Parallel chunks help use your connection effectively, including Premium benefits through your existing session. Actual speed depends on Telegram, the connection, your computer and disk.

Choose a disk with enough space and keep the computer, browser and Telegram tab open. Backups keep files on your disk and do not upload or delete them automatically. Only accessible history and supported attachments can be saved; review warnings.

Install or update

Enable Developer mode in brave://extensions or chrome://extensions, click Load unpacked and choose the folder containing manifest.json. To update, stop downloads, replace files in the same installation folder, reload the extension and refresh Telegram. Open the panel with Download at the bottom right.
